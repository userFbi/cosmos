01How long does production take?

05-07 business days for normal production. For a rush fee we can make it within 02-03 business days and ship!

02Can my graphics be etched into the stock chain?

No, we don't offer that on our stocks. All graphic work is a vinyl on top of the metal.

03How long to make a fully custom premier chain?

After artwork is approved and the deposit is received (1/2 of the invoice to start production), normally 03-04 weeks — not 12 weeks like other companies.

04How much do fully custom premier chains cost?

We can email you a price guide because there are many variations, but they range from $450 to $1300.

05How much for shipping?

Shipping varies by destination, but it's low and insured. We're in Ohio and generally ship UPS — UPS Ground may be as low as $16.99 with insurance. We also ship internationally; contact us for prices.
